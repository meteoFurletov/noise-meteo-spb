# Outlier Diagnostic Status

Using |Ri| > 10 as the outlier threshold, the validation sample has 143 two-sided outliers, 342 sounding-only outliers, 421 ERA5-only outliers, and 4282 samples where both sides are within bounds. That is a mixed one-sided pattern rather than one dataset alone failing: sounding-only is 6.6%, ERA5-only is 8.1%, and two-sided is 2.8%. Sounding-side outliers are not caused by interpolation/extrapolation from a too-high lowest reported level: among 485 sounding outliers, the median lowest usable height is 0.0 m, the maximum is 2.0 m, and 0 require extrapolation to 2 m. ERA5-side outliers are strongly associated with tiny 10 m to 1000 hPa shear: median delta-U is 0.66 m/s for ERA5 outliers versus 2.31 m/s for ERA5-sane samples, and 28.9% of ERA5 outliers have delta-U < 0.5 m/s versus 0.2% of ERA5-sane samples. Restricting the continuous comparison to samples where both |Ri_sounding| <= 10 and |Ri_era5| <= 10 leaves N=4282 and changes Pearson r from 0.005 to 0.427, RMSE from 2394.4 to 2.46, and bias from 59.0 to -0.04. The bounded-sample categorical metrics remain close to the full sample: hit rate 77.7% versus 77.7%, false alarm rate 14.4% versus 12.4%. Recommendation for the paper validation section: report categorical and rank-based metrics as primary, report bounded |Ri| <= 10 continuous metrics as secondary, and explicitly document bulk-Ri numerical instability under near-calm or near-zero-shear conditions as a property of the formula.
