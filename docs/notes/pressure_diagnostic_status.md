# Pressure Diagnostic Status

Across all 8036 paired timestamps, ERA5 surface pressure is below 1000 hPa in 16.2% of cases (DJF 27.7%, MAM 15.1%, JJA 8.3%, SON 13.7%). After applying the finite-Ri validation mask used by `agreement_statistics()`, only 13/5188 samples (0.3%) remain underground (DJF 0.5%, MAM 0.3%, JJA 0.1%, SON 0.1%); 1,285 of 1,298 low-pressure paired timestamps have non-finite sounding Ri. The full validation set has N=5188, Pearson r=0.005, Spearman r=0.680, RMSE=2394.4, bias=59.0, hit rate=77.7%, false alarm rate=12.4%, and miss rate=22.3%. The clean subset with surface pressure at or above 1005 hPa has N=5167, Pearson r=0.005, Spearman r=0.682, RMSE=2399.2, bias=59.2, hit rate=77.7%, false alarm rate=12.2%, and miss rate=22.3%. The underground subset has only N=13 finite samples, with Pearson r=0.992, Spearman r=0.615, RMSE=157.0, bias=44.2, hit rate=100.0%, false alarm rate=45.5%, and miss rate=0.0%. Removing underground hours does not bring the continuous metrics to the sensible target of Pearson r > 0.4 and RMSE < 5 Ri units, so the underground-pressure mechanism is not the dominant pathology and a second issue exists. Because the finite validation sample has very few underground cases even in DJF, the DJF-vs-JJA hit-rate disparity is not explained by underground 1000 hPa levels; the recommendation is to reconsider before committing to the 950/925 hPa download and adaptive-level refit.
